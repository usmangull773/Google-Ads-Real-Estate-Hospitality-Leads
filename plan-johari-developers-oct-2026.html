01

What we found

A public review of sandbankvillas.com, without account access. The site already has the right tools in place: GTM, GA4, Google Ads and the Meta pixel. The gap is that none of them can see which enquiry became a qualified buyer, so bidding has nothing to learn from except form fills.

Two Google Ads tags and a hard coded GA4 tag run beside the GTM container

High impact

The site loads AW-11471579855 and AW-10999048153 directly in the page code, plus GA4 (G-TH7D999G6Z), while GTM-5XKJZ847 is also installed. If any of these are also fired from GTM, or both Ads tags record the same lead, conversions are counted twice and the numbers will never match Pipedrive.

The click ID is lost between the ad and Pipedrive

High impact

Enquiries come through Squarespace forms and a Pipedrive Scheduler embedded in an iframe on the schedule a call page. No GCLID field was found on these pages, and tags on the site cannot see what happens inside the scheduler. So when a lead moves to qualified or reservation in Pipedrive, there is no link back to the click that produced it. It is also worth confirming the scheduler sits in the Pipedrive workspace your team owns, so the import runs from your own CRM.

1

Clean the tags and set one conversion per lead type

Confirm which Ads account is live, remove the duplicate tag, move GA4 and Ads into GTM only, and set form, booked call, WhatsApp and phone as separate actions. Only qualified outcomes are primary.

Why: bidding stops reacting to double counted or low value actions.
2

Carry the click ID into Pipedrive

Add hidden GCLID, GBRAID, WBRAID, country and UTM fields to the Squarespace forms and map them to custom fields on the Pipedrive deal. Turn on enhanced conversions for leads so scheduler bookings can be matched by hashed email.

Why: every deal can be traced to the campaign, keyword and country that produced it.
3

Import Pipedrive stages back into Google Ads

Map stages such as Qualified, Call held and Reservation to conversion actions with values, uploaded on a daily schedule. Qualified lead becomes the bidding signal; raw form fills become secondary.

Why: Google learns which searches bring buyers, not which bring the cheapest form fill.
4

Group markets into a few clusters so bidding has data

$3,000 split across many single country campaigns leaves each one starved. Countries are grouped by buyer value and language into two or three campaigns, with a small brand campaign. Rental, holiday and job searches are blocked with shared negative lists so hotel guests and villa buyers are kept apart.

Why: enough conversions per campaign for Maximize conversions now, and target CPA once qualified volume supports it.
5

Report by lead creation month, with market quality

A short weekly note your team can read without marketing terms: spend, leads, qualified rate and stage progress by country, with leads counted in the month they were created. Clarity recordings feed the first landing page test with your web team.

Why: budget moves toward the markets that produce deals, not just the cheapest leads.
